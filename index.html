PackIt

A gear library and trip packing planner for camping, overlanding and fish camp.

Catalogue what you own — weight, packed size, cost, condition — and record where it actually lives, down to which bin inside which container on which shelf. Build a trip from kits you already have, and let the prep and shopping lists work themselves out.

Everything stays on your phone and backs up to your own iCloud. There is no account and no server.
